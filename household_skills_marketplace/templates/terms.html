Terms & privacy

What this platform is

Household Skills Marketplace connects people offering household skills with clients. We do not employ providers, guarantee work, or process payments (version 1). Agree prices and safety arrangements directly.

Your information

We store the details you give us (name, e-mail, phone, profile text, messages and bookings) only to run the service. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Profile information you publish is visible to visitors. We e-mail you about account security, bookings, applications and matching jobs. Contact us to correct or delete your data.

Acceptable use

Be honest in your profile, be respectful in messages, and do not post unlawful or misleading content. Administrators may suspend profiles or accounts that break these rules.
